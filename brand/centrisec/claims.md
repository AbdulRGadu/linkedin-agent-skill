# Centrisec claims policy

## Safe claim types
- describe documented product capabilities
- explain security concepts
- describe the problem a feature is designed to address
- use approved public evidence from the proof library

## Require explicit evidence before publishing
- percentages or ROI
- customer counts
- revenue or growth
- named customers
- breach prevention claims
- guaranteed risk reduction
- compliance certification claims
- superiority claims about competitors

When evidence is missing, ask for it or remove the claim.
